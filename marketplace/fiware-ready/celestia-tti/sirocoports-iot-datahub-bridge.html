FIWARE-Ready: Celestia TTI - SirocoPORTS IoT DataHub-Bridge (Communication Networks,Data Analytics,Data Handling,Internet of Things,Sensory Systems)

SirocoPORTS IoT DataHubBridge is a modular, Docker-based middleware platform that enables seamless ingestion, decodiing, transormation, and publication of sensor and AIS data into NGSI-compliant FIWARE platforms.

Plug & Play integration with distributed LoRaWAN sensors and AIS sources. Native support for multiple FIWARE data models (WeatherObserved, AirQualityObserved, WateQualityObserved, etc.) Dockerized and scalable, includes MQTT broker and LoRa network server (ChirpStack) Real-time data pipeline from field devices to FIWARE and InfluxDB/Grafana Customizable and extensible, ideal form smart ports and industrial IoT deployments.

Modern port environments are increasingly dependent on real-time data from a wide array of IoT sensors and miritime systems. However, sintegrating heterogeneous data sources such as LoRaWAN sensor networks, AIS maritime traffic, and edge devices into a standarized smart data platform poses significant challenges - especially in terms of interoperability, data harmonization, scalability, and on-premise security. SirocoPORTS IoT DataHub-Bridge addresses these challenges by acting as a robust middleware component that collects, decodes, normalizes, and publishes data from distributed devices using NGSI-compliant formats. It bridges the gap between low-level device communication and high-level FIWARE context management, making it easier to deploy smart applications in complex and critical infrastrutrues like ports.

SirocoPORTS IoT DataHub-Bridge has been developed and validated within the framework of the SirocoPORTS 4.0 innovation project, funded by Puertos del Estado (Spain), with the direct collaboration of multiple port authorities. Pilot customers and validation partners include: Port Authority of Huelva Port Authority of Sevilla Port Authority of Santander Port Authority of Bay of Algeciras Port Authority of Las Palmas These ports have served as facilitators and testbeds for the operational use of the DataHub-Bridge in real world conditions.

No awards received to date. The solution is currently under evaluation in real port environments and is a candidate for future recognition within smart port and industrial IoT innovation programs.
