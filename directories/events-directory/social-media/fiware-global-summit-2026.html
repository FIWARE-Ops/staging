FIWARE Global Summit 2026

The FIWARE Global Summit 2026 returns to Europe — this time to the sun-drenched shores of Menton on the French Riviera. For almost a decade, the Summit has brought together the community behind FIWARE. This year, the focus is on that foundation — the technology itself, the people who use, advance, and champion it, and the conversations that move the ecosystem forward. Two days of technical sessions, workshops, and deep community exchange await. Come ready to connect, contribute, and shape what comes next.
